01 — About Me

Connecting systems,
at enterprise scale.

My journey into cloud technology began in July 2024, when I had just secured an engineering role at Cognizant while still completing my bachelor's degree. By day, I was learning enterprise technologies and adapting to the expectations of a professional environment. By night, I was attending classes, preparing for exams, and working toward graduation. Balancing both worlds was far from easy, but it taught me discipline, resilience, and the value of continuous learning.

A few months later, I successfully completed my degree and finished my initial training at Cognizant. What started as a curiosity about cloud technologies quickly turned into a passion. I joined my team as an Azure Integration Developer and began contributing to real-world business solutions, learning something new with every project, challenge, and deployment.

Since then, I have had the opportunity to work on large-scale enterprise integrations, helping connect systems, automate processes, and deliver reliable cloud solutions. My experience spans event-driven architectures using Azure Service Bus & Event Grid, data integration through Azure Data Factory, API governance with Azure API Management, and cloud-native development across the Microsoft Azure ecosystem.

Today, I continue to explore new frontiers in technology, including Azure OpenAI, AI Agents, Kusto Query Language (KQL), observability, and Infrastructure as Code. I believe the best engineers never stop learning, and I am committed to growing one step at a time while building solutions that create real value.
